Spotify queue bot

Built: forward the song via WhatsApp and the bot appends it cleanly. No interruptions, no hijacked speaker - it has survived every party since.

Cloud-9

Spot instances are cheap - until the wrong choice interrupts a workload or quietly costs more.

Built: a cloud optimizer ranking EC2 spot instances by price and interruption risk, forecasting prices with ML, and generating deployable infrastructure.

open the live product ↗

Agentic CX operations reporting

Management reporting from live planning data is repetitive, time-consuming, and too important to leave to free-form model output.

My contribution: turned an existing internal prototype into the deployed version on SAP BTP Cloud Foundry with the Agents SDK. Agents retrieve live Jira data; a deterministic pipeline validates and renders the final reports.

AI planning assistant

All the planning context sits in Jira, and there was no API to it - so every dependency question turned into an afternoon of manual digging.

Built: a semester-planning assistant for the Business Suite Portfolio Management team. It reads the Jira data from a SQL mirror and runs purpose-built report and dependency workflows on top of it.

check24-MCP

Comparison portals have data everywhere and no coherent interface for AI agents.

Built: one MCP surface across CHECK24 verticals, with host-specific adapters, conformance checks, routing, and observability - deployed on Cloudflare Workers. Won the CHECK24 GenDev scholarship.

read the repository ↗

Predicting pyrolysis yields

Lab experiments are expensive; the relationship between process conditions and product yields is hard to see.

Built: Random Forest and XGBoost models on 795 literature datapoints, grouped by paper to prevent leakage and checked with SHAP against the physics.
